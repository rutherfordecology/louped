About the data
  • Species records are public GBIF records in the area, at the location GBIF publishes. Records whose location was hidden by their source, or could be more than 5 km off, are left out. Records cluster where people go, so few records means few visits, not few species.
  • Click anywhere on the map to see every layer at that spot.
