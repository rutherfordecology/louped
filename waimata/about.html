About this map

Waimata catchment biodiversity map

An interactive map for the Waimata catchment group and landowners: the public information about the catchment's land, water and wildlife, in one place. It shows layers to turn on and off, not a score or ranking.

Using the map

  • Layers. Turn layers on and off with the eye, and fade them with the slider. Under land cover, classes lets you show only some kinds of cover, such as native vegetation.
  • What's here. Click anywhere to see every layer at that spot, and any species records nearby.
  • Your block. Draw an area around a property or block to get its species list, land cover and protected areas, and download them.
  • Species. Species are grouped; click a group to open it. Tick species to show them on the map, click a name to see only its records, and use the camera (or Photos in LookLike) to see photos.

The catchment

The area is the Waimata River catchment above where the Taruheru joins it in Gisborne, traced from NIWA's river network (REC2). Check the boundary with the catchment group. Area: 22,440 ha.

Species records

3,365 public records of 523 species from GBIF.org, snapshot 2026-10-27. By licence: CC BY 2,587 (77%), CC BY-NC 749 (22%), CC0 29 (1%).

  • Records are shown where GBIF publishes them. Nothing is blurred on this map.
  • Left out: records whose location was already hidden by their source (for example iNaturalist obscures threatened species to about 28 km), records that could be more than 5 km off, records with no name, and sea species recorded at the coast (Jasus edwardsii, Evechinus chloroticus, Haliotis iris, Prionace glauca, Cookia sulcata, Maoricolpus roseus).
  • Records cluster along roads, tracks and towns, where people go. Few records means few visits, not few species.
