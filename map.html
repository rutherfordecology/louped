Species records

    About the data
    • Species records are every public GBIF record in the area, at the location GBIF publishes. Some records are already blurred by their source (for example iNaturalist hides threatened species); their circle shows how far off they could be. Records cluster where people go, so few records means few visits, not few species.
    • Canopy height is the LINZ LiDAR surface model less the ground model (2023), shown at 4 m when zoomed in and 16 m when zoomed out. Height change is against the 2018–20 survey; changes under 2 m are left clear.
    • Land cover is LCDB v6 (2023). Wetlands are Gisborne District Council's provisional regional wetland assessment (2022), not all field-checked. Land use capability is NZLRI. Land Overlays 1–3 and 3A are the council's erosion-susceptibility overlays.
    • Click anywhere on the map to see every layer at that spot.
